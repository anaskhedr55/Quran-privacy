Privacy Policy

1) Data Stored On Device

  • The app stores local settings, reading/listening progress, bookmarks, favorites, download records, memorization (Tasmee‘) history, and optional local account data for on-device sign-in.
  • All of this data remains on the user's device and is not transmitted to the developer.

2) Personal Data Collection

  • The app does not collect, store, or share personal data with the developer or with third parties.
  • No personal information such as name, email address, or contacts is collected by the application.
  • If you enable location permission, location is used only to show your city and calculate Qibla and prayer times for you inside the app and widget.
  • Location is requested only once on first launch; after approval your city, coordinates, and timezone are saved locally so the app and widgets keep working offline without asking again.
  • A full year of prayer times is computed and cached locally on device so widgets refresh without any network connection.
  • Location data is processed locally on device and is not shared with the developer for analytics, ads, or tracking.

3) Microphone & Voice Memorization (Tasmee‘)

  • The Memorization (تسميع) feature uses the microphone only while you are actively reciting, and only after you grant Microphone and Speech Recognition permission.
  • Speech is processed using Apple’s Speech Recognition. On-device recognition is used when your device supports it, so your audio never leaves the device.
  • When on-device recognition is unavailable, audio may be processed by Apple’s speech service under Apple’s privacy policy solely to transcribe your recitation. The developer never receives or stores your audio.
  • Recitation audio is never recorded to a file, uploaded to the developer, or shared with third parties. Only your local memorization progress (surah, ayah, completion %, accuracy, date) is saved on device.
  • You can revoke Microphone or Speech Recognition permission at any time in iOS Settings.

4) Apple CarPlay

  • When connected to CarPlay, the app shows surahs, reciters, and Now Playing controls so you can listen to the Quran safely while driving.
  • CarPlay uses the same on-device data and audio sources as the app and does not collect additional personal data.

5) Tracking & Analytics

  • The app does not use tracking technologies, advertising identifiers, analytics services, or advertising SDKs.

6) Network & Content Sources

  • Recitations are loaded from external providers including mp3quran.net and related servers.
  • Official Mushaf pages and Quran resources are provided by King Fahd Complex for the Printing of the Holy Quran.
  • Quran text used for memorization tracking is bundled in the app from the King Fahd Complex Hafs dataset and is matched against your recitation entirely on device.
  • Location permission depends on system location services to provide city name and local prayer-time calculations.
  • When online content is requested, these providers may process technical data such as IP address under their own privacy policies.
  • Provider privacy reference: https://www.mp3quran.net/ar/privacy
  • King Fahd Complex reference: https://qurancomplex.gov.sa/privacypolicy/

7) Downloads Logic (Offline Audio)

  • Downloads start only when the user taps "Download Surah" or "Download All Surahs".
  • Local metadata may include reciter ID/name, surah number, download status, timestamps, and local file reference.
  • On native iOS/Android builds, completed audio files are saved inside app-private storage for offline playback.
  • On native builds, offline audio downloads are not limited by a fixed in-app storage cap. Storage availability depends on device free space and OS behavior.
  • On web builds, downloads are handled by the browser download manager.
  • Deleting a download removes the local record and attempts to delete the local file on native builds.

8) Data Retention & Deletion

  • Most data remains locally on the user's device until the user deletes it inside the app or uninstalls the app.
  • Users may remove local account data, download records, and memorization history from within the application.

9) Children

  • The app is intended for general audiences and is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13.

10) Copyright & Source Notice

  • Recitations and audio streams remain the property of their original rights holders.
  • According to King Fahd Complex policy, website content is protected except services, computer files, or software tools explicitly made available for public use.
  • Source: https://qurancomplex.gov.sa/privacypolicy/

11) Policy Updates

  • This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
  • This version (2.0.0) adds the Memorization (Tasmee‘) voice feature, Apple CarPlay support, and an offline yearly prayer-time cache.

  • عند تفعيل إذن الموقع، يُستخدم الموقع فقط لإظهار المدينة وحساب اتجاه القبلة ومواقيت الصلاة لك داخل التطبيق والويدجت.
  • يُطلب الموقع مرة واحدة فقط عند أول تشغيل، وبعد الموافقة تُحفظ مدينتك وإحداثياتك ومنطقتك الزمنية محليًا ليستمر التطبيق والويدجت في العمل بدون إنترنت ودون طلب الإذن مجددًا.
  • يتم حساب مواقيت صلاة سنة كاملة وتخزينها محليًا على الجهاز لتحديث الويدجت بدون اتصال بالإنترنت.
  • تتم معالجة بيانات الموقع محليًا على الجهاز، ولا تتم مشاركتها مع المطور لأغراض التتبع أو الإعلانات أو التحليلات.

3) الميكروفون وتسميع الحفظ

  • تستخدم ميزة التسميع (الحفظ) الميكروفون فقط أثناء تلاوتك، وبعد منحك إذن الميكروفون والتعرف على الكلام.

  • يمكنك سحب إذن الميكروفون أو التعرف على الكلام في أي وقت من إعدادات iOS.
